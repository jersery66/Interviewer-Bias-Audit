## 2026-07-03 — pre-execution freeze

- The user-supplied v2 plan was frozen before model execution.
- Primary cohort is restricted to official AVEC train + dev (142 participants; PHQ-8 >= 10; 43 positive, 99 negative). Official test is excluded from every primary fit, tuning, calibration and inference step.
- Canonical five-condition source is the read-only verified package at `F:\数据库\DAIC-WOZ\processed_research\verified_source_data_official142_20260703\03_canonical_text_official142\mpnet_conditions_official142_verified.csv`. File hashes and a copied input snapshot will be recorded before analysis.
- The historical TF-IDF specification is frozen as lowercase Unicode-normalized word 1–2 grams, `min_df=2`, `max_features=50000`, `sublinear_tf=True`, followed by balanced liblinear logistic regression with `max_iter=2000` and `C=1.0`.
- Outer splits reproduce the historical main-analysis rule: ten separately seeded stratified 5-fold partitions with `BASE_SEED=20260624` and repeat seeds `BASE_SEED + repeat` for repeat 1–10. Every train/test membership row is persisted.
- Inner split seed is deterministic and outer-fold-specific: `BASE_SEED + 1000 + 100*repeat + fold`; all tuning, threshold selection and calibration remain inside the outer training set.
- Threshold candidates are midpoints between sorted unique inner-OOF probabilities plus 0 and 1. Ties are broken by distance to 0.5, then by the lower threshold. `sensitivity_at_spec80` maximizes sensitivity subject to specificity >= 0.80, then specificity, then the same tie rule.
- Participant-level nested decisions use the mean across repeats of `outer_test_probability - outer_fold_selected_threshold`; nonnegative mean margin is positive. This is algebraically equivalent to comparing each participant’s mean probability to their mean selected threshold and avoids mixing fold-level summary metrics with participant-level OOF inference.
- Platt and isotonic calibrators use only outer-training data with the same three inner folds. Isotonic results are supplementary.
- Five-bin quantile ECE permits fewer than five bins when tied probabilities collapse quantiles; effective bin count is always recorded.
- Primary input-source FDR family contains all ten C1–C5 pairs for four predeclared endpoints: ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Macro-F1@0.5 and Macro-F1@nested. Sensitivity, specificity and calibration metrics are descriptive unless explicitly listed in a family table.
- `gte-Qwen2-7B-instruct` is pre-replaced before execution by `BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5`: the available GPU has 6 GB VRAM, while a 7B model cannot be run in ordinary FP16 within that limit, and the current Python model environment is CPU-only. The two non-selectively reported dense models are therefore `sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2` and `BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5`, each pinned to a full Hub revision before encoding.
- Frozen Hub revisions resolved before encoding: `sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2@e8c3b32edf5434bc2275fc9bab85f82640a19130` and `BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5@d4aa6901d3a41ba39fb536a557fa166f842b0e09`. ## 2026-07-03 — C4 control definition audit

- The broad canonical `is_protocol_template_spoken` flag cannot support the planned within-participant non-template length/position controls: in the official142 cohort it marks most interviewer speech as template, leaving fewer non-template words than template words for every participant with interviewer speech and zero non-template words for many plain-format transcripts.
- The C4 removed-template target is therefore frozen as the canonical `is_explicit_clinical_prompt == 1` turns, while non-template controls use `is_explicit_clinical_prompt == 0` interviewer turns. This directly matches C4’s definition as interviewer speech after explicit clinical prompts are removed. All 142 participants have enough non-explicit text to match the explicit-prompt word count; participants 451 and 458 have zero interviewer/template words and remain explicit all-zero/empty cases.
- Template-presence IDs are deterministic IDs assigned to sorted unique normalized explicit-clinical-prompt texts. This encodes observed prompt coverage/counts only; it is not an Ellie symptom-domain-question indicator.
- Dense inputs use 200-word chunks with 50-word overlap to prevent silent truncation. Main aggregation is the unweighted mean of normalized chunk embeddings followed by document L2 normalization, matching the v2 plan’s default mean pooling. Empty documents map to an all-zero vector and are listed.
- Dense logistic-regression `C` is selected inside each outer training fold from `[0.01, 0.1, 1.0, 10.0]` by mean inner ROC-AUC; ties choose the smaller `C`. Scaling is fit only on training data.
- C5 random controls use ten fixed seeds `20260704` through `20260713`. Tokens are sampled without replacement from participant speech and restored to source order to match the reviewed-evidence word count exactly. The nonsymptom pool excludes all exact reviewed-quote token spans before sampling; exclusions and shortages are audited.
- C5 keyword masking is case-insensitive, whole-token masking of a frozen English list stored in the run configuration; masked tokens are replaced by `[MASKED]` rather than deleted to preserve word count.
- C4 template definitions come from the canonical turn-level field `is_explicit_clinical_prompt` and turn position; deterministic template IDs are assigned from sorted unique normalized explicit-prompt text. Length-matched controls sample non-template interviewer tokens to the exact template word count. Position-matched controls greedily select the nearest unused non-template interviewer turns, then trim the final turn to the target count. Template shuffling is performed separately inside outer train and outer test partitions and each mapping is saved, preventing cross-fold text transfer.
- No prior numerical result is imported as an analysis result. Earlier scripts and audits are used only to recover the historically frozen TF-IDF specification and to identify known reproducibility failures that the new tests must prevent.
